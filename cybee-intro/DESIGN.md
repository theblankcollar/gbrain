# Cybee Intro — Design

Placeholder brand identity for Cybee (www.cybee.ai). Content was not reachable
at authoring time, so brand copy below is generic AI-agent copy — swap with the
real hero when available.

## Style Prompt

Bold, energetic product intro with a cinematic cyber-brand edge. Dark canvas
tinted with deep indigo, hot magenta accent glow behind the wordmark, an
electric blue secondary accent for supporting type. The title card reads as
confident and kinetic — fade-in choreography with slight upward drift and a
letter-spacing expand on the wordmark, held against a muted background video
with a subtle vignette. Motion is decisive, short, and varied-eased (expo.out
for the hero, power3/power2 for support).

## Colors

- `#050514` — background (near-black, indigo-tinted)
- `#FFFFFF` — foreground text
- `#FF006E` — primary accent (hot magenta, hero glow)
- `#3A86FF` — secondary accent (electric blue, eyebrow + underline)
- `#B9BFD6` — muted foreground (subtitle, body)

## Typography

- `Inter` 900 — brand wordmark (expanded letter-spacing on reveal)
- `Inter` 500 — eyebrow (tight uppercase with wide tracking)
- `Inter` 400 — tagline and subtitle

## What NOT to Do

- No cyan-on-dark or purple-to-blue gradient text fills.
- No left-edge accent stripes or generic card grids.
- No linear full-screen gradients behind the wordmark — use a soft radial glow.
- No pure `#000` background — keep the indigo tint.
- No exit animations before the final fade — the intro ends on the title.
